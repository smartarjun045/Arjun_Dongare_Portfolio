How A Data Project Works

1. Clean & Explore

Audit raw data, resolve formatting issues and uncover baseline trends with Python, SQL or Excel.

2. Model & Visualize

Build interactive Tableau or Power BI dashboards, train baseline ML models or apply structured analytics workflows.

3. Report & Deliver

Deliver clean visualizations, documented notebooks or Flask dashboards ready for stakeholder review.

Project scope, timelines and deliverables depend on dataset availability and requirements. Forage job simulations (Deloitte Australia, Tata Group) and YuvaIntern training represent structured learning experiences rather than formal employment.
